You're offline

The app itself is saved on this device, but your books live on the server and need a connection to load. Reports, balances and sending an invoice all wait for signal — nothing is made up on this phone.

Field capture is the one exception: time entries, receipt photos and expense notes you capture offline are saved on this phone and listed in the sync panel — every one of them, by name — until they send. They are not on your books until the panel says they sent.

Reconnect — job-site signal counts — and tap below; the app will pick right back up, fully current.
